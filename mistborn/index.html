A thousand years of tyranny have settled over the land like soot. Skaa toil under noble houses that burn metal for power they were never meant to share, and something moves in the mists at night that the histories don't mention. The Ashen Crown follows a crew of misfits, mistings, and worse who've decided the Final Empire has stood long enough.

The World

The Final Empire

For a thousand years the Lord Ruler has held the empire together through fear, ash, and the noble houses that answer to him. Skaa work the plantations and forges under a caste system enforced by the Steel Ministry and its Obligators, while the nobility burn allomantic metals in private and call it birthright.

Ash falls from the sky most days. The sun sits dim and red behind the haze. Every night, the mists roll in and something about them has started to feel less natural than the histories claim — and lately, more than one crew has gone missing in them.
