Rules

Below you will find how we are going to run this campaign. Feel free to return here to reference if you have any questions.

⚠️ These rules are subject to change if we need to balance something. Keep in mind, this was homebrewed from scratch — some things may need balancing over time.

Step 1 — Choose Your Pokémon

You can choose your Pokémon by taking the quiz, or by picking for yourself from the starter list. Players who stick with their quiz result will receive a bonus.

  • Quiz: nrosa01.github.io/pmd-quiz-online ↗
  • Starter List: serebii.net/dungeonsky/starter.shtml ↗
Step 2 — Roll Your IVs

IVs give you a bonus to your stats. An IV value can be up to 31. To roll your IVs, roll 5d8 and drop the lowest (if you roll a 32, treat it as 31). Do this 7 times, then drop the lowest total. You may assign these values to any stats you please.

Step 3 — Determine Your Stats

There is a calculator built into this site — enter your Pokémon, level, and IVs to get your stats. Newly created Pokémon start at Level 5.

Step 4 — Figure Out Your Moves

See each Pokémon's page for their level-up moves and what they do.

Step 5 — Choose Your Ability

Each Pokémon has more than one ability (including hidden abilities). Choose whichever you like — depending on the ability, it may function differently than it does in the mainline games.

2.1 — General
  • At the start of a dungeon, everyone rolls initiative. This determines the order in which everyone moves throughout the dungeon.
  • Dungeons are random — the structure changes every time you enter.
  • Once you enter a dungeon, there are only 3 ways to leave:
    • Fainting (see Section 3.2)
    • Clearing the dungeon (reaching the stairs on the final floor)
    • Completing a Job
2.2 — Exploring
  • Out of combat, initiative is not as strict. Characters can move up to 30ft on their turn as long as no enemies are after them.
  • Parties can only advance to the next floor as a group. If a member is not present, the party cannot move on.

When you advance to the next floor, the following happens:

  • You cannot go back to the previous floor.
  • Your Hunger decreases by 10.
  • You regain HP equal to your HP modifier.
  • You are healed of any special conditions.
  • All stat changes end.
2.3 — Weather

Some dungeons will be in harsh environments, and bad weather may have negative effects on certain floors. Some moves can also cause weather for 5 turns.

Rain Water moves do 50% more damage; Fire moves do 50% less damage.
Downpour Water moves do 75% more damage; Fire moves do no damage.
Harsh Sun Fire moves do 50% more damage; Water moves do 50% less damage.
Drought Fire moves do 75% more damage; Water moves do no damage.
Sandstorm All types except Rock, Steel, and Ground take 2 damage every turn. Rock, Steel, and Ground types have their Special Defense modifier increased by 50%.
Hail All types except Ice take 2 damage every turn. Ice type moves do 50% more damage.
3.1 — Attacking

On your turn, you get 1 move action and 1 standard action.

  • Move action: Move 5ft in any direction (including diagonals).
  • Standard actions: Perform any attack, use an item, move an ally (5ft with consent), or move an extra 5ft.
To Hit: Roll 1d20 + the category modifier (Attack or Sp. Attack), DC 12. For every 5 points of Accuracy below 100, add 1 to the DC (e.g. a 95 Accuracy move is DC 13).
Damage: You get one die per half of your modifier (rounded up) based on the move's power. A 40-power move uses a d4, with one die per half of your Attack or Sp. Attack modifier.
Example: A Rattata using Tackle (40 power) with a +4 Attack modifier would roll 3d4 instead of 2d4.
  • STAB: For any move that matches your type, roll 50% more dice.
  • Super Effective: 2× the damage rolled.
  • Not Very Effective: ½ the damage rolled (rounded down).
  • Double Weakness / Resistance: 4× damage or ¼ damage respectively.
  • If you run out of PP, you cannot use that move until you leave the dungeon or use a PP restoring item.
  • Basic Attack: Deals damage equal to your Attack Modifier.
  • Struggle: Roll 1d4 per Attack or Sp. Attack modifier, then take 75% of that as recoil.
  • Stat changes (positive or negative) last for 10 turns, or until you step on a Reset Tile.
  • Critical Hits occur on a Natural 20 (no confirm). Critical damage is always double the number of dice rolled.
3.2 — Fainting
  • If you faint, you lose 15% of the money on you, 1d6 items (chosen randomly) from your bag, and ALL treasure chests on you. These are not dropped — they are lost to the dungeon and cannot be recovered.
  • If you have a Reviver Seed in your bag, it can be consumed automatically. You will not lose any items besides the seed itself.
  • If you faint without a Reviver Seed, your teammates have 3 turns to revive you with a Reviver Seed or Revive Orb.
3.3 — Conditions

Certain moves apply special conditions. Some persist until healed by an item or by advancing to the next floor.

Paralysis For up to 4 turns, you cannot move. You can still attack and use items. DC 15 Speed check to end it early.
Sleep For up to 3 turns, you cannot move or attack. Ends when you take damage or an ally within 5ft uses an item to wake you. DC 15 Defense check to wake early.
Poison Every 3 turns, take 5% of max HP in damage. When you take damage, make a DC 15 HP check to end the condition.
Bad Poison Every 3 turns, take 10% of max HP in damage. When you take damage, make a DC 15 HP check to end the condition.
Burn Every 2 turns, take 1d4 damage and your Attack modifier is halved. When you take damage, make a DC 15 Attack check to end the condition.
Frostbite Every 2 turns, take 1d4 damage and your Sp. Attack modifier is halved. When you take damage, make a DC 15 Sp. Attack check to end the condition.
Confused Any movement or attack is performed in a random direction (roll a d8). At the end of your turn, roll a Sp. Defense check to end your confusion.
Flinch You cannot perform any attacks next turn.
Disabled Your last move cannot be used until you advance to the next floor or remove this condition with an item.
Infatuated 50% chance to be too in love to act on your turn. No turn limit, but make a Sp. Def check DC 13 each turn to end it early.
  • You may only hold 12 jobs at one time.
  • The number of items you may hold in your bag is determined by your Exploration Team Rank (see Section 5).
  • You will gain 12 EVs every level. You may put these in any stat, but no single stat may exceed 252 EVs.
  • Once EVs have been allocated, you cannot respec them.
