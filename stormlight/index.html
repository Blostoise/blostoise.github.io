OATHBOUND
A Homebrew D&D 5e Campaign

The storm is coming.
Will you bond the light?

Somewhere on the Shattered Coast, gemhearts are cracking open early, chasmfiends stir before their season, and ordinary people are waking up bonded to spren they don't understand. Oathbound follows one warband of the newly Radiant as they're pulled into a war that started centuries before they were born.

Roshar, Reforged

A world shaped by storm and stone

Everything here grows a shell, roots itself against the wind, or dies. Highstorms scour the land every few days, chasms split the plains into a maze, and the old magic — stormlight — is waking back up.

The Highstorms

Every few days a wall of wind and lightning rolls across the continent. It charges spheres with stormlight, floods the chasms, and kills anyone caught in the open.

The Shattered Plains

A maze of wind-carved chasms and rock plateaus, home to chasmfiends, bridge crews, and the warcamps still fighting a war nobody remembers starting.

The Radiants Return

Shardblades are waking. Ordinary people are hearing spren speak for the first time in centuries — and swearing oaths they don't yet understand the weight of.

Before You Sit Down

House rules

Rules this table has agreed on, on top of standard 5e. Update freely as your campaign evolves.

Stormlight as a resource

Infused spheres light the warcamps and fuel Radiant abilities. Track your stormlight like a consumable — it drains over time and during a highstorm's absence.

Swearing the Ideals

Radiant progression is tied to roleplay, not XP thresholds alone. A new Ideal is sworn at the table, out loud, when it's earned.

Highstorm timing

Highstorms strike on a rough real-world schedule the GM tracks between sessions. Getting caught outside during one is a serious threat, not flavor text.

Table safety tools

Lines, veils, and an open-door policy on stepping away from a scene apply every session. Speak up any time, no explanation required.

Want a seat at the table?

We run every other week. New players are welcome between arcs — reach out and we'll find you an oath to swear.
